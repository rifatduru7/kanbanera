## What the timesheet shows

The timesheet view summarizes work logged during the current week. It is designed for review rather than planning.

## Weekly summary

At the top of the page, the current week’s total duration is aggregated into a single summary value.

## Daily breakdown

Each day can show:

- total tracked time
- the date
- individual task-linked entries
- duration per entry

This lets users understand where time went without leaving the weekly context.

## Best practice

Track time from the task you are actually working on. That keeps the reporting layer tied to real delivery work instead of generic manual entries.
