## What the Members page is for

Members is the fastest way to manage who can access a specific project and what they are allowed to do there.

## Core actions

On the Members page, admins and owners can typically:

- choose the active project
- invite people by email
- assign a role during invite
- change roles later
- remove members from the project

## Role model

The project role set includes:

- owner
- admin
- member
- viewer

The owner role is special and should remain limited. Project admins should also be granted intentionally rather than by default.

## Access review habits

Review project membership when:

- a contractor leaves
- a review phase ends
- the ownership model changes
- a stakeholder no longer needs visibility

## Principle to follow

The right access model is the smallest one that still lets people do their job.
