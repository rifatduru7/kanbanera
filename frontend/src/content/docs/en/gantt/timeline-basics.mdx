## Why use the Gantt view

The board shows workflow state. The Gantt view shows time risk. You need both if you are planning around deadlines.

## What makes a task visible

Tasks need meaningful dates to appear in the timeline. If dates are missing or unrealistic, the chart becomes misleading quickly.

### Overdue signals

Overdue work matters most when it is still assigned and still active. Use the timeline to catch slippage before it compounds.

## Review the chart regularly

A weekly schedule review is usually enough for stable teams. Fast-moving teams may review the chart daily.
