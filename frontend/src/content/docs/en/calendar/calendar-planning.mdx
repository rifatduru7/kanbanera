## What Calendar is best at

Calendar is the fastest way to answer one question: what is due when?

Use it when the team needs to review delivery distribution across days and weeks rather than detailed task status movement.

## Available planning views

Calendar supports:

- month view for broader scheduling
- week view for short-term planning
- mobile-friendly agenda behavior where applicable

## Quick add workflow

Calendar includes a quick-add path for creating tasks directly from a selected date. This is useful when planning is date-led rather than board-led.

For quick add, choose:

- project
- column
- title
- due date

## Filters

Use filters to isolate the schedule by:

- project
- priority
- assignee

This is especially useful for planning reviews, personal deadline checks, and coordination meetings.

## When to use Calendar vs Gantt

Use Calendar when:

- you care about due-date distribution
- you want quick weekly or monthly planning
- you need to add dated tasks quickly

Use Gantt when:

- timeline shape matters more than calendar slots
- you need zoom levels and grouped review
- schedule movement should happen directly on a timeline
