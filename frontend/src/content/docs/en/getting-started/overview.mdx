## Workspace structure

Era Kanban keeps day-to-day execution inside a single authenticated workspace. The core layers are:

- projects for grouping work
- board for daily task flow
- gantt for schedule visibility
- automations for reducing repetitive actions
- portal for external approvals and client feedback

## How teams usually work

Most teams start in the board view, move into Gantt for planning, and use automations once the workflow stabilizes.

### Board-first execution

Board is the fastest way to create, assign, comment on, and move tasks between statuses.

### Timeline visibility

Gantt adds due dates, dependencies in planning terms, and a broader picture of delivery risk.

### External review

Portal gives you a controlled space for review and revision without opening the full dashboard.

## Recommended first steps

<Steps>
  <li>Create a project with a clear owner.</li>
  <li>Define the columns your team actually uses.</li>
  <li>Add real tasks with assignees and due dates.</li>
  <li>Open the Gantt view and validate the timeline.</li>
  <li>Add one automation only after the manual process is clear.</li>
</Steps>
