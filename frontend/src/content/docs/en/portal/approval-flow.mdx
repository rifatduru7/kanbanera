## When to use the portal

Portal is designed for people who need to review work but should not operate inside the complete project dashboard.

## Approval outcomes

An approval flow should end in one of two outcomes:

- approved
- revision requested

### Capture precise feedback

Revision requests should explain what must change, not just that something feels incomplete.

## Connect portal review to execution

When feedback is actionable, convert it into task updates, comments, or subtasks so the next assignee can execute quickly.
