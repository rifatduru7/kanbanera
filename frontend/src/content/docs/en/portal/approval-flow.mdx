## Why Portal exists

Portal gives you a controlled review surface for clients, stakeholders, or approvers who should not work inside the full dashboard.

## What reviewers can do

Portal supports review actions such as:

- reading task context
- leaving comments
- approving work
- requesting revision

## Approval outcomes

The cleanest approval process has two explicit outcomes:

- approved
- revision requested

Anything else creates uncertainty and slows handoff.

## Reviewer input quality

Revision notes should explain what has to change, not just that something is wrong. Good feedback reduces follow-up loops.

## Internal follow-through

Portal feedback should not stay in the portal mentally. Convert it into:

- updated task descriptions
- comments for the working team
- subtasks for revision items

## Notifications and traceability

Approval and revision events also feed the wider notification and task workflow, so the operational team can react from inside the dashboard.
